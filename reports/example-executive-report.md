# Example Executive Exception Report

> Synthetic demonstration only. Values do not represent any organization.

## Portfolio Summary

The synthetic portfolio illustrates six remediation-exception scenarios: active, high-risk/expiring, expired, policy-invalid, remediated/validated, and low-risk active. The purpose is to demonstrate how exception governance exposes residual risk and approaching deadlines rather than suppressing vulnerabilities.

## Executive Observations

- One synthetic critical exception combines a critical vulnerability, critical asset, external exposure, and known-exploited context; this would warrant senior review and accelerated remediation.
- One record is intentionally missing an accountable owner and should fail policy validation.
- One exception is expired and should be escalated rather than silently extended.
- One record demonstrates evidence-backed closure after remediation validation.

## Management Actions

1. Prioritize critical/high exception reduction.
2. Require explicit ownership and approval evidence before acceptance.
3. Review all exceptions approaching expiry within 30 days.
4. Track repeat renewals as technical/security debt.
5. Require authoritative validation before closure.

## Interpretation

Exception counts alone are insufficient. Risk should be considered alongside exploitability, asset importance, compensating controls, expiry pressure, and remediation validation status.
